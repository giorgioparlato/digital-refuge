# quotes for review

About 107 candidate quotes on attention, sacredness, time with people, nature, awe and enchantment, for a possible home-screen add-on. **None of these are in the app yet.** Tick the ones you want (`[x]`), or delete lines, and I'll add only those.

**How each quote was checked** (the tag after the source):

- **text:** matched word for word against the full public-domain text (Project Gutenberg).
- **translator:** matched on the translator's own page (accesstoinsight.org, dhammatalks.org) or in the original paper.
- **book:** confirmed with the book's title on Goodreads or several quote sites, but not checked against a printed page.
- **check:** the author and work are real, but the wording varies between sources or I couldn't pin down the exact book. Check these before using them.

**Notes:**

- **Case.** The app shows all text in lowercase, so these will appear as "the land knows you, even when you are lost."
- **Length.** Quotes marked *(long)* may be too long for a phone's home screen.
- **Copyright.** The Thoreau, Emerson, Müller Dhammapada and Lemont Rilke texts are public domain. The modern ones are short excerpts from copyrighted books, including the translations by Barks, Mitchell and Thanissaro. That is normally fine as brief quotation, but worth knowing for a public app.
- **Left out on purpose:**
  - **Clichés:** "I went to the woods…", "quiet desperation", "the wound is the place where the light enters you" and "one wild and precious life".
  - **Misattributions:** "It's not what you look at that matters, it's what you see" is not Thoreau.
  - **Lines the author was quoting from someone else:**
    - Goodreads lists a James Baldwin line under Hari.
    - It lists the Xinxin Ming ("when love and hate are both absent…") under Burbea.
    - It lists William James under Odell. James is credited properly below.
